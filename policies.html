Shipping & Returns

Shipping & Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days. Shipping times vary depending on the product, warehouse location, and carrier. Estimated delivery windows will be displayed at checkout when available.

Once your order ships, you will receive an automated email confirmation containing tracking details. Murnan Auto is not liable for carrier delays or packages delivered to incorrectly provided shipping addresses once an order has been dispatched.

Cancellations & Modifications

Because items enter fulfillment quickly with our logistics partners, requests to modify or cancel an order must be made as soon as possible by emailing support@murnanauto.store. Once an order has entered fulfillment or shipped, it cannot be canceled and must be processed as a standard return.

Returns & Exchanges

If you need to return an item, contact us within 30 days of delivery at support@murnanauto.store to initiate a return request and obtain return instructions.

  • Do not send items back without authorization. You must email support first to receive the correct return warehouse location and authorization instructions.
  • Items must be unused, uninstalled, and in original packaging with all included hardware and manuals.
  • Custom, made-to-order, or print-on-demand merchandise (such as apparel) is non-returnable unless defective or damaged in transit.
  • Return shipping fees are the responsibility of the customer unless the shipment arrived defective or incorrect. Original shipping charges are non-refundable.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your package arrives damaged or you receive an incorrect part, contact us within 5 business days at support@murnanauto.store with your order number and photo evidence of the damaged or wrong item so we can arrange a replacement.
