Shipping & Returns

Shipping & Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days. Shipping times vary depending on product stock, warehouse distribution centers, and carrier schedules. Estimated delivery windows displayed at checkout are estimates and not guaranteed delivery dates.

Once your order ships, you will receive an automated email confirmation containing tracking details. Murnan Auto is not liable for carrier delays, lost packages marked as delivered, or packages delivered to incorrectly provided shipping addresses once an order has been dispatched.

Freight & Oversized Deliveries

Large or heavy items (such as full exhaust systems, bumpers, or crate components) may ship via LTL freight carrier. You must carefully inspect the external box and contents for damage BEFORE signing the bill of lading (BOL) or delivery receipt. If damage is visible, note the damage on the receipt with the driver or refuse the shipment. Failure to inspect freight prior to signing releases the carrier and Murnan Auto from freight claim liability.

Cancellations & Modifications

Because items enter fulfillment rapidly with our logistics partners, requests to modify or cancel an order must be made as soon as possible by emailing support@murnanauto.store. Once an order has entered processing at a distribution facility or has shipped, it cannot be canceled and must be processed as a standard return.

Returns & Restocking Fees

If you need to return an item, contact us within 30 days of delivery at support@murnanauto.store to initiate a return request and receive official return instructions.

  • Do not send items back without authorization. You must email support first to receive an RMA number and the correct distribution warehouse address. Shipments returned without authorization will be rejected.
  • Items must be brand new, unused, uninstalled, and in original packaging with all included hardware, documentation, and accessories. Installed parts are strict non-returnable.
  • A standard 15% to 20% restocking fee may apply to authorized returns to cover warehouse handling, processing, and distributor costs.
  • Custom, made-to-order, or clearance items are non-returnable unless defective or damaged in transit.
  • Return shipping costs are the sole responsibility of the customer unless the error was made by Murnan Auto or the carrier. Original outbound shipping charges are non-refundable.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your package arrives damaged or you receive an incorrect part, contact us within 5 business days at support@murnanauto.store with your order number and clear photos of the packaging, labels, and product. We will work directly with the manufacturer or supplier to issue a replacement.

Privacy Policy

All payments are processed through encrypted third-party payment gateways (Stripe). Murnan Auto never stores, processes, or retains your full payment card details or financial bank credentials on our servers.

How We Use & Share Your Information
